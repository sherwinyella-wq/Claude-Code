Our Story

◆
September 2019

How We Met

A mutual friend's rooftop birthday party in New York City. Emma was laughing at something on the far side of the roof when James spotted her. He spent the next forty minutes finding an excuse to wander over. The rest, as they say, is history.

March 2020

Our First Adventure

A spontaneous weekend trip to Vermont — planned in 48 hours. They got hopelessly lost hiking, found a tiny diner that made the best waffles either of them had ever eaten, and drove home in a snowstorm singing along to a playlist they made together on the spot.

December 2022

Moving In Together

After two and a half years of splitting time between apartments, they finally signed a lease on a little brownstone in Brooklyn. Moving day involved three broken boxes, a stray cat that followed them inside, and a celebratory pizza eaten on the floor.

October 2025

The Proposal

James proposed on a quiet evening walk along the waterfront in Charleston — a city they'd fallen in love with on a trip the previous spring. With the sun going down and their dog Biscuit present as the only witness, Emma said yes before he could finish the question.

June 14, 2026

The Wedding

We can't wait to celebrate with the people who mean the most to us. Thank you for being part of our story.
